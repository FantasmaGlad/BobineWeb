Features

What Bobine changes day to day in your gym

A playout system that runs on its own: classes start on time, your setup backs itself up, and members help themselves without needing you.

Video playout

Your classes start on their own, on time

No more staff having to press play at the right moment: Bobine plays every session on schedule, in 1080p or 4K, with no stutter or freeze, even on a small refurbished computer.

Technical details
Resolution & frame rate
1080p and 4K up to 60 frames per second
CPU utilization
Under 8% CPU usage during active video playout
Supported codecs
H.264, HEVC (H.265), VP9, AV1, WebM
Dual displays
Main wired HDMI output plus secondary networked stream
Backup & updates

Your setup backs itself up and updates itself

A mistake or a failing drive doesn't have to mean starting over: export your entire configuration in one click, and restore it in seconds on a new machine.

Technical details
Backup
Full ZIP export (video library, schedule, settings)
Restore
One-click import with SQLite integrity checks
Factory reset
Requires an explicit typed confirmation
Updates
Automatic check for new releases on GitHub
Members

Members help themselves

Between scheduled classes, the room stays alive: a member picks a session on the touch screen or scans a QR code to start it from their phone, with nothing to install and no need to ask for help.

Technical details
Kiosk UI
Touch-optimized full-screen interface for fast browsing
Mobile remote
Local Wi-Fi QR code scan with no app installation needed
Wireless remotes
Plug-and-play USB air-mouse / remote support
Response time
Local WebSockets, near-instant reaction
Ambiance & reliability

An atmosphere that never stops

Continuous background music outside of classes, and a machine that comes back on its own after a power cut or restart, with nothing for you to do.

Technical details
Audio crossfade
Smooth transitions with zero audio silence gaps
Scheduled spoken alerts
Automated announcements (gym closing, safety guidelines)
Systemd watchdog
Continuous process supervision with automatic recovery
Power cut resilience
Instant automatic reboot and resume after power cuts
