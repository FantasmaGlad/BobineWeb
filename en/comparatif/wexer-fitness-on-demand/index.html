Frequently asked questions

Does Wexer rely only on streaming?

No. Virtual Player documentation describes local storage and offline playback of classes already downloaded.

Is Bobine latency guaranteed?

It depends on hardware, files and the local network: since videos play directly from local disk without cloud buffering, start-up is immediate on a modern Mini PC or Mac.

Methodological note & neutrality

These comparisons are written by the Bobine project, with no affiliation to the named brands. Offerings change: read the official sources and confirm terms with each supplier. Bobine is cross-OS playout software and does not sell a licensed workout catalog.
